# Assets and where they come from

- **Exercise animations (`public/exercises/<slug>/loop.webp`, `poster.webp`)**: rendered by us with `scripts/exercise-renders` in Blender.
  The body is the male figure from Blender Studio's *Human Base Meshes* bundle (v1.4.1), released under CC0 (public domain), with the face
  replaced by our own smooth head. The machines, benches, dumbbells and bars are modelled in the scripts from simple shapes. No third-party
  textures or models besides that mesh.
- **Exercise photos (`public/exercises/<slug>/0.jpg`, `1.jpg`)**: from the free-exercise-db dataset (Unlicense). The dataset does not say where
  its photos came from, so they are being replaced by the renders above as each lift gets one.
- Fonts (Archivo, Figtree) are self-hosted; no third-party scripts, fonts or analytics.
